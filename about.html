About Me

A machine-learning developer from Alexandria who enjoys turning difficult ideas into clear, usable products.

Origins

My Story

I was born and raised in Alexandria, Egypt — a city that shaped my love for both heritage and innovation. I attended Sidi Gaber Language School where I met the people who would become my brothers; they introduced me to coding and showed me the art of problem-solving.

That spark of creativity is why I chose this field. I enjoy the full journey: understanding a problem, testing the data, improving the model, and shaping a web experience that helps another person use the result confidently.

Outside project work, competitive programming keeps my thinking disciplined, while design and storytelling help me communicate technical decisions without making them feel complicated.
